# desk_16 — Literature review and prioritized research plan (2026-09-24)

This extends `docs/RESEARCH.md` (Parts I–III, 2026-09-21), which already covers target engineering, ML model families, 8-K metadata, peers, crowding, buffers and HRP. Most of those ideas were already tested and killed (docs/EXPERIMENTS.md). This review targets what desk_13–15/17 found:
1. The book is a persistent quality/profitability/value tilt, and its returns are style-factor returns.
2. dtc does not replicate out of sample; the SI cap does.
3. Cross-sectional dispersion predicts the composite's IC.
4. Text only works outside the universe.

Every idea below records its source, the method, why it could apply here, and the hypothesis it motivates. Evidence grade: **A** = peer-reviewed primary source; **B** = working paper or practitioner research from a reputable shop; **C** = secondary summary.

## Ideas considered and rejected before testing
- **Value-spread timing** (L4 + desk_17 non-replication).
- **Book-level volatility targeting** (L7 + crowding_gate).
- **LLM-based text reading**: the brief discourages it without specific evidence, and prior agent architectures were discarded. Nothing new motivates it.
- **More tree models on the 18 factors** (composite_prior, era_split, recency_window all killed).
